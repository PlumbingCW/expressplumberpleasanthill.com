If you have a burst pipe or active leak before we arrive

Shut off the main

Turn the main water valve clockwise until it stops. Look near the meter, along the front of the house or in the garage.

Kill power to wet areas

If water is near outlets, switch off those breakers only if you can reach the panel on dry floor.

Clear the area

Lift rugs, electronics and boxes off wet floors, and keep children and pets out.

What changes between Concord neighborhoods

Concord was incorporated in 1905, and the page history shows the town grew through the 1940s to the 1960s. That means many homes carry plumbing from the postwar decades, including galvanized supply lines and clay or cast iron drains that have had decades to corrode or take in roots. Downtown, around Todos Santos Plaza, newer apartment and condo buildings add shared stacks and risers, which fail differently from a single-family lateral.

Near Buchanan Field and along the corridor toward the Concord Pavilion, many businesses depend on working restrooms and hot water, so our commercial plumbing crews handle those calls too.

Concord ZIP codes we serve

Our primary Concord ZIP is 94520, the downtown ZIP that includes the Todos Santos Plaza area. We also cover 94518, 94519 and 94521, which together make up the city. Whichever of these your address falls in, the same 24/7 line applies.

Older lines under Concord streets

Interstate 680 and Route 242 shorten the drive into Concord

Pleasant Hill sits close to Interstate 680 and State Route 242, and both give quick routes into Concord from the west and south. Short drive times matter when a tank is flooding, and arrival still depends on dispatch and traffic.

Work in Concord follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and the City of Concord decides what permits apply within its limits.
