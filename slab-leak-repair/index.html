Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Pleasant Hill, CA

A slab leak is a pressurized water line leaking beneath or inside the concrete floor of your house, and it gets worse while you decide what to do. The Pleasant Hill line is open 24/7: shut off the main valve, keep electronics off the floor and call.

Slab leak repair means locating the failed section of pipe under the slab and fixing it by spot repair through a small opening, by rerouting the line overhead, or by replacing the line.

Why a slab leak is an emergency

Water under a slab can undermine soil, lift flooring and feed mold. A hot water line leaking under a floor wastes energy as well as water, and some homeowners notice the warm spot before they notice any wet.

If your meter is moving with every fixture off, and the floor is warm or damp, shut off the main valve and call at any hour. We confirm the leak with a listening device before anything is cut.

Warm patch on the floor

A localized warm spot in a room with no vent or heater is a classic sign of a leaking hot line.

Sound of running water

A faint hiss with all fixtures off, heard with an ear near the floor.

Low pressure

Pressure that drops at taps with no explanation can mean a leak upstream.

Cracks and damp carpet

New cracks in tile or damp baseboards near a wall point toward water under the floor.

Three ways to repair a slab leak

OptionHow it worksBest when
Spot repairBreak a small section of slab, repair the pipe, patch the concreteOne accessible leak and otherwise sound pipe
RerouteAbandon the slab line and run new pipe through walls or atticThe line is old, or leaks keep returning
ReplaceReplace the entire supply loopWidespread corrosion or multiple leaks

Rerouting avoids breaking the slab and gives you a line you can reach later. It is common in older homes with failing copper. Our repiping page covers whole-house replacement when more than one line is failing.

Slab homes in the Pleasant Hill area

Many postwar tract homes around Contra Costa Boulevard were built on concrete slabs with pipes running beneath. Over decades, those lines can leak at joints or where they rub the concrete. The age of the house is a clue, not a diagnosis, and we locate the leak rather than guess.

If you see damage on a floor, first take photographs and move furniture. Leak detection covers the tools we use.

What affects slab leak repair cost
