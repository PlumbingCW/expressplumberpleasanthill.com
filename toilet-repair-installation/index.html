Emergency Toilet Repair in Pleasant Hill, CA

An overflowing toilet is an emergency at 2 a.m. as much as at noon, so the Pleasant Hill line is answered 24/7. Lift the tank lid, push the flapper down, or turn the shutoff valve behind the bowl clockwise to stop the water, then call.

Toilet repair means fixing a clog, a running tank, a leaking base or a cracked bowl, and toilet installation means setting a new toilet on a sound flange with a fresh seal.

Toilet overflow emergency: stop the water first

1

Reach the shutoff

The oval valve on the wall or floor behind the toilet. Turn it clockwise.

2

Lift the float

If the shutoff is stuck, take the tank lid off and lift the float arm or rod to stop the fill.

3

Do not flush again

A second flush adds water to a bowl that is already full.

4

Call us

If every toilet and tub is backing up, the main line may be blocked. See sewer line cleaning.

When to replace instead of repair

Repair is usually enough when

  • The tank or bowl is not cracked
  • Only the flapper, fill valve or seal failed
  • The toilet is relatively young
  • The flush works when the part is fixed

Replace when

  • The bowl or tank is cracked
  • The toilet clogs often
  • Parts are no longer available
  • You want a more efficient fixture

Modern toilets use less water per flush than older ones. California requires new toilets sold in the state to meet water efficiency standards, and a replacement may lower your water use compared with a toilet from the 1950s or 1960s.

Installation done on a sound flange

  • We check the flange for cracks, rust or a poor fit, because a damaged flange makes any toilet rock and leak.
  • We set a new wax or wax-free seal and tighten the bolts evenly.
  • We replace the supply line and shutoff valve if they are corroded.
  • We test flush and check for leaks before we leave.
  • We haul away the old fixture.
