Emergency Sewer Line Repair in Pleasant Hill, CA

Sewage coming up through floor drains, several fixtures backing up at once, or a gurgling toilet when you run the washer: the lateral may be broken, rooted or collapsed. The Pleasant Hill line is answered 24/7, and a visit can be same day when available. Stop using water until the plumber arrives.

Sewer line repair is fixing a broken, offset, root-damaged or collapsed section of the pipe that carries wastewater from your house to the public sewer, either by digging down to it or by trenchless methods.

Sewer backup: what to do first, and how we tell it from a break

A clog can be cleared with a cable. A broken pipe cannot. When a line backs up again within days of cleaning, or a cable hits a hard stop at the same distance every time, we send a camera down the pipe to see the cause.

The video shows roots, a bellied section holding water, an offset joint, or a crushed section. We show it to you and explain what repair fits.

What we see on cameraWhat it meansTypical repair
Roots at a jointOpen joint letting roots inClear, then repair or reline the joint
Bellied sectionSoil settled under the pipeReplace the low section
Offset jointPipe sections have shiftedDig and realign, or reline if mild
Collapse or crushPipe failed structurallyExcavate and replace
Clear, round pipeBlockage was the issueClean and monitor

Dig or trenchless?

Open trench

  • Works for almost any damage
  • Lets us replace and regrade
  • Disturbs landscaping or hardscape
  • Often the choice for collapsed pipe

Trenchless

  • Pipe bursting or lining with small access pits
  • Less lawn and driveway damage
  • Needs a pipe the method can pass through
  • Not suited to every failure

Trenchless is not always possible, and we tell you plainly when it is not. A camera pass tells us. See sewer inspection for details on what we record.

Who owns which part of the sewer

Central San states that the property owner is responsible for the entire private sewer lateral, from the house to the connection at the public main, including the part under the street. Service calls on the public main are handled by the district, and a lateral repair is handled by the owner.

Central San also states that it has permit authority and inspection jurisdiction for building sewer repairs in its service area, that exterior sewer work needs a district permit before any trenching or excavation, and that the contractor must be registered with the district. Inspections need 24 hours notice before work starts. We confirm those steps before opening the ground.
