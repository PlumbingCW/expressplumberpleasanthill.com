Whole-House Repiping in Pleasant Hill, CA

Repiping replaces the water supply pipes in your house when they are old, corroded or leaking repeatedly. In Pleasant Hill, many homes date from the postwar decades, and we help owners decide whether to repair or replace with real numbers.

A repipe removes the existing supply lines and installs new pipe, usually copper or PEX, to every fixture, with new shutoffs, then patches the access openings.

Signs your pipes may be at the end

Repeated pinhole leaks

A second or third leak in a few years means the pipe wall is thinning along its whole length.

Rusty or brown water

Rust-colored water from the tap that clears after running points toward corroding steel.

Low pressure

Galvanized pipe narrows from the inside as scale builds, and pressure falls at fixtures.

Stains and damp spots

Discolored drywall or a musty smell near walls may mean a slow leak.

Postwar plumbing: what we may find

Pleasant Hill's growth took off after the war, with the post office opening in 1948 and the Gregory Gardens subdivision developed in 1950. Houses built then often had galvanized steel supply lines, and some were later partly repiped in copper.

We do not assume. A camera and a pressure test, and a look inside the walls and crawlspace, tell us what material you have. Mixed systems with galvanized and copper joined together corrode at the joints, so they are worth a closer look.

Repair or repipe: a decision guide

A repair makes sense when

  • The leak is a single event
  • The rest of the pipe tests sound
  • The pipe is relatively young
  • You are about to sell and need a stopgap

A repipe makes sense when

  • Leaks keep returning
  • Water quality or pressure is poor
  • Pipes are galvanized and old
  • You plan to stay in the house long term

What the job involves

1

Survey and plan

We map the lines, fixtures and access routes, and discuss copper or PEX.

2

Permit

We confirm the permit your city requires for repiping.

3

Replace

We install new lines, usually through the attic or crawlspace, with minimal wall openings.

4

Test and patch

We pressure test, restore water and patch drywall openings. Painting is separate.

Plan on having the water off for part of the day. We schedule the work so you have a toilet and a tap available where possible. If a single failing line is the issue, our burst pipe repair page covers a smaller fix.
