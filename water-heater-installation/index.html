Water Heater Installation in Pleasant Hill, CA

Water heater installation in Pleasant Hill is the planned side of the work: a remodel, a new addition, a switch from tank to tankless, or a unit you want in place before the old one fails. We quote the job before we start and schedule it around your week.

A water heater installation places a new heater, connects water, gas or power, adds the safety devices code requires, and tests the system before it goes into service.

Where installation differs from replacement

Replacement is a swap in the same spot. Installation often moves the heater, changes its fuel or adds a second unit. A garage tank moved to an exterior wall, or a tank swapped for a wall-hung tankless, changes gas sizing, venting and sometimes electrical supply.

If your tank has already failed, our emergency replacement page covers that urgent path. This page is for jobs you can plan.

Safety details inspectors look for

The California Plumbing Code governs how a heater is installed. The City of Martinez's water heater handout summarizes the items, and we use it as a checklist. It calls for two straps on a tank, with a third above 52 gallons, fastened to a stud with a lag bolt, and it prohibits plumbing tape as strapping.

It also lists a minimum 12-inch vent termination above the roof, 4 feet from any building opening, and a gas shutoff ahead of the sediment trap. Those details are the same regardless of city, so they are the standard we build to.

Installing in older homes

Postwar homes in the Gregory Gardens area and along Contra Costa Boulevard often have attached garages with the heater beside the furnace. Moving or replacing the unit can trigger a vent upgrade, and old galvanized shutoff valves sometimes break when touched. We budget for those surprises up front.
