A water heater failure is an emergency

A tank that leaks through its shell can release dozens of gallons before anyone notices, which is why the first minutes matter. Once the water is off, the questions move to what size and fuel type you want, and whether your venting and gas line are ready for it.

We handle both in one visit when possible. A crew arrives, confirms the size and type with you, and gives a clear price before work starts. After-hours calls are answered the same way, so you are not waiting until a showroom opens.

Tank, tankless or heat pump hybrid

Traditional tank

  • Lower upfront cost
  • Familiar installation
  • Limited hot water supply per cycle
  • Standby heat loss

Tankless or hybrid

  • Hot water on demand with tankless
  • Less standby loss
  • Higher upfront cost
  • May need gas line, venting or electrical upgrades

Heat pump water heaters are a growing option in California, and some installations qualify for utility or state incentives. We do not promise a rebate, because programs change, so check the current offers before you buy. Our tankless water heater page explains sizing and maintenance for on-demand units.

What a proper replacement includes

  • Shutting off and draining the old unit, then hauling it away.
  • New flexible water connectors and a shutoff valve on the cold line.
  • A temperature and pressure relief valve with a drain pipe that ends 6 to 24 inches above the ground, per the code items in the City of Martinez handout.
  • Seismic straps: two straps, plus a third on tanks over 52 gallons.
  • A gas shutoff valve and flexible connector, or a correct electrical disconnect.
  • Checking the vent for proper rise and clearance to combustibles on gas units.
  • Testing for leaks and gas tightness before we leave.

The Martinez handout also notes that every water heater installation needs a permit, and replacements can be issued over the counter. Pleasant Hill may handle this differently, so ask the Pleasant Hill building division. We will tell you what paperwork your job needs before we start.

Planning around your home

Garages are the most common location for tanks in this part of Contra Costa County, and the code asks that gas burners sit at least 18 inches above the floor unless the unit is rated for flammable vapor resistance. Closets and attics need extra attention for venting and drip pans.

If your new unit is bigger than the old one, we check that the space, the gas line and the vent can handle it before ordering.

What drives the cost of a replacement

Your price depends on the unit and the home. The usual factors:

Unit type and sizeA 40-gallon tank and a tankless system are priced very differently.
Venting and gasMoving a vent or upsizing a gas line adds labor and materials.
Code upgradesStraps, relief drains, expansion tanks and shutoffs may need to be added.
Permit and inspectionFees and timing depend on the jurisdiction.
Removal and accessAttics and tight closets take longer than garages.

Insurance generally covers the water damage from a sudden tank failure but not the replacement tank. Ask your insurer before cleanup begins.
