Emergency Water Line Repair in Pleasant Hill, CA

When the pipe that brings water from the meter to your house breaks, you may see a wet yard, hear water running with every tap off, or lose pressure entirely. The Pleasant Hill line is open 24/7: close the shutoff at the house, check the meter, and call.

Water service line repair is fixing or replacing the underground pipe between the utility's meter and your home, which is typically the owner's responsibility from the meter onward.

Signs the service line is failing

Wet or sunken ground

A soggy patch or sinkhole in the yard between the meter and the house, with no rain or irrigation to explain it.

Pressure drop

Pressure that falls throughout the house at once, not only at one fixture.

Meter spinning

With every tap off, the meter still moves. See leak detection for how we confirm this.

Discolored water

Brown or rusty water after a main break or repair work can point to a service line problem.

Why a service line break is urgent

A leaking service line is under full street pressure, so it can wash out soil, flood a crawlspace or driveway, and run up a bill quickly. If you hear a rush of water or the lawn is flooding, close the valve at the house, and if that does not stop it, tell us so we can bring the right tools to shut off at the meter.

Only the utility can operate its own meter valve. We tell you who to call and handle the repair on your side.

Repair, replace or reroute

1

Locate

We trace the line and pinpoint the break or the failing section.

2

Choose a method

A short break can be spot repaired. A long run of old pipe is usually replaced.

3

Dig or pull

Open trench, or pipe bursting with small access pits when the route allows.

4

Test and restore

We pressure test the line, reconnect to the house and restore the area.

Older homes sometimes have galvanized steel service lines that corrode inside until the flow drops. Replacing the whole line with modern pipe is often better than patching. The repiping page covers the inside of the house.

Planning around a yard or driveway

Before any digging we call 811 for underground utility marking. We also look at trees, driveways and sprinkler lines that sit on the route. Trenchless replacement can protect a driveway or patio when the old pipe route is clear.

What affects service line repair cost

We give a clear price before work starts. Factors:

Length of the lineA short run to the meter costs less than a long one.
Depth and soilRocky or clay-heavy ground slows digging.
Surface to restoreConcrete and pavers cost more to repair than lawn.
MethodTrench and trenchless are priced differently.
PermitsSome agencies require them for service line work.

Some homeowners policies offer optional service line coverage. Check before you start work.
