Emergency Drain Cleaning in Pleasant Hill, CA

A backed-up drain does not wait for business hours, so the line is answered 24/7 for Pleasant Hill homes and shops. While you wait for the plumber, stop running water, leave the toilets alone if a tub or floor drain is gurgling, and keep kids and pets away from standing sewage.

Drain cleaning is the removal of a blockage from a household drain line using a cable machine, a hydro-jetter or a hand tool, followed by a camera check when the clog keeps coming back.

When a clogged drain is an emergency

A slow bathroom sink can wait until morning. A toilet that bubbles when the washing machine drains, a tub that fills with gray water, or sewage rising in a floor drain cannot. Those signs point to a blockage in the shared line, and every flush or load of laundry sends more water into your house.

We treat that situation as an after-hours call at any hour, including weekends and holidays, and we book a same-day visit when a technician is free. The first step on arrival is to find which fixtures share the blocked section, because that tells us whether the clog is in a branch line or in the main line out to the street.

Stop adding water

Hold off on showers, laundry and dishwasher cycles. Every gallon you add has nowhere to go but the lowest opening in the house.

Do not mix chemicals

Skip the second bottle of drain cleaner. Caustic liquid sitting in a standing clog can splash on the technician and eats into old metal traps.

Find the cleanout

If you can locate the capped cleanout near the house or by the curb, tell us. It is usually the fastest place to start clearing the line.

Protect the floor

Move rugs and cardboard boxes away from low drains, and photograph any water for your insurance file before cleanup.

What your symptoms are telling us

One fixture drains slowly

Usually hair, soap film or grease in that fixture's own trap and branch. A cable or a small jet clears it without touching the rest of the system.

Two or more fixtures slow together

The blockage sits downstream where those lines meet. This is the pattern we see when a kitchen line and a bathroom line both back up in a ground-floor home.

Water rises in the lowest drain

A tub or floor drain that fills while a toilet flushes means the main line is restricted. Treat it as urgent and stop using water.

The clog returns every few weeks

Repeat clogs point at roots, a belly in the pipe or scale on old iron. That is the cue to run a camera instead of clearing the same blockage again.

How we clear it: cable, jet or camera

Most calls start with a cable machine, a motor-driven steel cable with a cutting head that bores through the clog. We choose the head size to match the pipe so the cable does not skip past the blockage or tear a hole through soft pipe.

If the cable keeps hitting resistance or comes back with roots, we step up to a hydro-jetting pass, which scours the pipe wall with high-pressure water. A short sewer camera inspection afterward shows whether the pipe is sound or needs sewer line repair.

Kitchen sink and dishwasher clogs

Kitchen lines clog because cooking fat cools inside the pipe and traps food particles. In many Pleasant Hill kitchens the sink, the dishwasher drain and the disposal all share one run, so a grease plug backs up all three at once.

We clear the line, then check the disposal outlet and the dishwasher's high loop, since a kinked hose can make a healthy line look blocked. If the disposal itself hums or leaks, our garbage disposal repair page covers that side of the job.

Why drains in older Pleasant Hill homes keep clogging

Pleasant Hill grew quickly after the war. The post office opened in 1948, and the Gregory Gardens subdivision was developed in 1950. In houses of that era, galvanized steel supply lines and cast iron or clay drain lines were common, and a plumber checks what is really in the wall instead of assuming.

Roots are the other big factor. Central San, the district that serves Pleasant Hill, calls root intrusion the leading cause of sewer backups and advises a professional inspection or cleaning at least every other year when trees stand near the lateral. That advice is worth following if your lot has mature trees along Contra Costa Boulevard or the Iron Horse Trail side streets.

Shower, tub and floor drains

Showers clog with hair and soap scum a few inches below the strainer, and a drain tool can usually pull it out in minutes. Tub drains that gurgle when a toilet flushes are the more serious case, because they tell us the shared line is partly blocked.

Floor drains in garages and laundry rooms are a quiet trouble spot. They often sit dry for months, then fail when the washer discharges, so we test them with a bucket of water before we leave.
