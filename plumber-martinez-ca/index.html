The downtown valley and Alhambra Creek

The town was laid out in 1849 and became county seat in 1850, which makes the downtown some of the oldest development in the county. Alhambra Creek runs through the densely built downtown valley, so older buildings often sit close to a creek bed, with soil that stays damp and lines that settle.

Old homes and storefronts here are the kind where clay or cast iron drains and early water lines can still be in service. A sewer camera inspection shows what is there, and sewer line repair covers fixes when the pipe has shifted.

Courthouse and downtown offices

Small offices and shops need hot water and working restrooms to stay open. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock. See commercial plumbing.

Waterfront and the Carquinez Strait

Damp air near the strait is hard on exterior fittings and hose bibs, so we check them on every outdoor call.

Permits and the county seat

Martinez publishes a water heater handout that cites California Plumbing Code items such as seismic straps and relief drains, and it notes that replacements can be issued over the counter. We use that handout as a checklist on every Martinez tank job and confirm the permit steps with you before we start. See our water heater replacement page for what a proper install includes.

Martinez ZIP code we serve

Martinez is covered by a single ZIP, 94553, which runs from the waterfront and courthouse area through the older neighborhoods and out toward Alhambra Valley. That one ZIP is also the page's primary ZIP, and the same 24/7 line answers for every address in it.

Refinery town, working town

Martinez has had refineries since 1904, with the Shell refinery opening in 1916 and still running. Many residents work shifts, so after-hours plumbing calls are routine here and not an inconvenience. We answer the same at 2 a.m. as at 2 p.m.

Work in Martinez follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and the city's own water heater handout lists the items its inspectors look for.
