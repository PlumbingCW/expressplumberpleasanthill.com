Emergency Leak Detection in Pleasant Hill, CA

A hidden leak does damage every hour it runs, so the Pleasant Hill line is open 24/7. If you hear water running with every tap off, or a wall is wet, shut off the main valve and call. We schedule same-day detection visits when available.

Leak detection is the process of finding where water is escaping a pressurized supply line, a drain or a fixture, using meters, listening devices and thermal imaging so the repair can be made with a small, targeted opening.

Signs that a leak is already costing you

Water meter moves with taps off

Close every fixture, check the meter dial or low-flow indicator, and watch for movement. Movement means water is going somewhere.

Bill higher with no change in habits

A sudden jump on one cycle is a clue. Compare it with the same months from past years.

Damp spots or a musty smell

Staining on a ceiling, bubbling paint or a mildew smell near a wall points to a slow leak behind it.

Warm patch on a floor

A warm spot on a slab can mean a leaking hot water line beneath it. Read our slab leak page for next steps.

Treat it as an emergency when

Active dripping inside a wall, water coming through a ceiling, or a meter that spins fast are all reasons to shut off the main valve and call right away. Mold can start in a damp cavity within days, so quick action saves drywall and insulation.

If the leak is visible and from a fixture, a shutoff valve under the sink or behind the toilet is usually enough. If you cannot find the source, shut the main and leave the rest to the tools.

Find the main valve

Most homes have a shutoff near the water meter or where the line enters the house.

Photograph the damage

Dated photos help with an insurance claim.

Do not open walls

Cutting drywall blindly makes a repair bigger. Let the locator point to the spot.

Note your meter reading

Writing it down before and after an hour with all water off helps confirm a leak.

Common sources in older Pleasant Hill homes

Many houses here date from the postwar building boom, when galvanized steel and early copper were common. Old galvanized pipe corrodes from the inside and leaks at threaded joints, and copper can pit where water chemistry is aggressive.

Ask your water utility about its water quality report if you suspect corrosion. We do not quote a hardness or pH figure for your address because we have not verified one.

What to do before the locator arrives

  • Close the main if water is flowing, and open one tap to relieve pressure.
  • Do not cut into drywall to look for the source.
  • Move belongings away from damp areas and take photos.
  • Write down the meter reading and the time.
