Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Pleasant Hill, CA

When a main line is clogged, every drain in the house is affected, and the Pleasant Hill line is answered 24/7. Stop using water, find your cleanout if you can, and call. A sewer line cleaning visit can often be booked for the same day when a crew is free.

Sewer line cleaning is clearing the private lateral between your house and the public sewer of grease, roots, wipes or debris, using a cable machine or hydro-jetter and confirming the result with a camera.

Main line backup: when it is an emergency

A single slow sink is a branch problem. A main line clog affects the lowest drains first, usually a tub, shower or floor drain, and gets worse when anyone flushes or runs the washer.

The fastest test is a cleanout. Many homes have a capped pipe near the house, along a side yard or at the front. If water stands in it, the blockage is downstream of that point. If it is dry, the blockage is between the house and the cleanout.

Stop water use

No flushing, laundry or dishwasher until the line is clear.

Find the cleanout

Look for a 3 or 4 inch capped pipe near the house or by the sidewalk.

Do not open it fully if full

A cleanout full of sewage will overflow if the cap is removed.

Keep people away

Keep children and pets away from standing wastewater.

Cable, jet, then camera

For a blocked lateral we start with a cable to restore flow. Because a cable can leave a coating behind, we recommend a hydro-jetting pass when roots or grease are the cause. We finish with a camera look so you can see whether the pipe is sound.

If the camera shows damage, we explain the findings and discuss sewer line repair. If the pipe is clear, we tell you so, and we suggest how often to clean.

Preventing the next backup

  • Keep cooking grease out of the sink. Let it solidify in a can and put it in the trash.
  • Flush only toilet paper.
  • Install a cleanout if your lateral has none.
  • Follow Central San's advice of an inspection or cleaning at least every other year if trees are near the lateral.
  • Avoid planting water-seeking trees near the line.
