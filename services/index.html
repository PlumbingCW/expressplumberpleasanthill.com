How we decide what you need

Most callers know the symptom, not the cause. A slow drain might be a hair clog or a root-filled lateral. A cold shower might be a pilot light or a failed tank. We start with a few questions, tell you what to check safely, and send a plumber who has the right tools for the likely cause.

If the likely cause is urgent, we book the earliest visit available, often the same day. If it can wait, we schedule a time that suits you. Either way, you hear a clear price before work starts.
