Where Sherman Acres is

Sherman Acres is a residential neighborhood in Pleasant Hill, an older residential neighborhood that includes Sherman Acres Park, a city park. Neighborhood maps place it close to Interstate 680 and State Route 242, with the Walnut Creek channel nearby. Public profiles list Sherman Acres under both the 94523 and 94520 ZIP codes. A shared ZIP code does not tell you which city an address belongs to, so use your street address to confirm.

One public profile lists the largest groups of housing units as built in the 1980s and the 1950s, which means older and newer houses can share a block.

Judge the house, not the neighborhood

On a street with a 1950s home next to a 1980s one, plumbing needs can differ even though the addresses are near. A newer house may have different pipe materials and a different water heater setup than its neighbor. We do not assume either. A short look at what you have is faster than guessing.

Common calls

A tank that no longer keeps up is a reason for water heater repair or water heater replacement. A drain that is slow in more than one fixture points to sewer line cleaning, and a camera sewer inspection shows the line's condition. A rising water bill with no change in use is a reason to ask about leak detection.

The sewer line

Central San says the property owner is responsible for the full sewer from the house to the main, including the part under the street, and that it has permit authority for building sewer repairs in its service area. If a backup keeps returning, get the line inspected before you decide on repair.

What to have ready when you call

  • Your street address, since a shared ZIP code does not settle which city a house belongs to.
  • Which fixtures are affected. One slow sink and several slow fixtures point to different causes.
  • The approximate year the house was built, if you know it.
  • Whether the main valve is closed and whether water is still moving.

Those four answers let us describe the likely cause and give a price range before a visit is booked. If more work shows up on site, it is shown to you and the price is revised before it continues.
