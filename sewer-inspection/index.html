Sewer Camera Inspection in Pleasant Hill, CA

A sewer camera inspection shows what is actually inside your pipe, so you stop guessing about roots, bellies and breaks. We offer it in Pleasant Hill for home buyers, owners with repeat clogs and anyone planning a remodel near the lateral.

A sewer camera inspection feeds a small waterproof camera on a flexible rod through a cleanout or fixture, recording video of the pipe and noting the depth and location of any defects.

When an inspection pays for itself

  • Before you buy a home, especially one built in the postwar decades.
  • When a drain clears but backs up again within weeks.
  • After roots have been cut once and returned.
  • Before landscaping, a driveway project or an addition near the line.
  • Before and after hydro jetting, to confirm the pipe is clean.

If the problem is urgent, see sewer line cleaning. If the camera finds damage, sewer line repair describes the fixes.

Inspections for home buyers

A general home inspection does not look inside the sewer lateral. If you are buying a home built in the 1950s or 1960s, ask whether the seller has a recent camera report. Pleasant Hill's postwar subdivisions, including Gregory Gardens, are the kind of neighborhoods where older clay laterals appear.

Because Central San places responsibility for the lateral on the owner, a defect you buy is a defect you repair. A camera visit before closing gives you leverage and a budget.

What an inspection cannot do

A camera cannot see through a full blockage, so we may clear the line first. It also cannot tell the soil conditions around the pipe, and it shows a moment in time. If a tree is growing near the line, repeat the inspection every couple of years, in line with the district's advice.

How to use the video

The footage is most useful when it is paired with the depth and location marks. If the camera shows a defect at 40 feet, the locator tells us where that spot is on the surface, so a repair can be planned without guesswork.

For a home purchase, share the video with your agent and, if you like, with a second plumber. A clear defect gives you room to negotiate a repair credit. A clean video gives you confidence, and you can repeat the inspection every couple of years if trees are growing near the line.
