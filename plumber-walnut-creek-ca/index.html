Three eras of Walnut Creek housing and the pipes in them

Before the 1950s boomOlder cottages near downtown with mixed repairsCorroded galvanized lines, clay drains with roots
1950s and 1960sPostwar tract homes, many with slabs or raised floorsPinhole leaks, slab leaks, aging water heaters
1970s onwardCondos, townhomes and larger subdivisionsShared stacks, water heater replacement, fixtures

Broadway Plaza opened in 1951 as Contra Costa County's first major retail center, and the page history places Walnut Creek's big growth in the same postwar window. The city had 34,251 housing units at the 2020 census, with about 64 percent owner occupied, so a lot of these houses have changed hands and been remodeled in pieces.

Remodels hide old plumbing. A new kitchen can sit on a supply line from 1955. That is why leak detection and a pressure test come before any quote on an older house.

Slab and crawlspace homes

Warm floors and a spinning meter point to slab leaks. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock. See slab leak repair.

Tanks past their prime

Garage heaters from the tract-home years. Same-day appointments when available. See water heater replacement.

Condos and townhomes

A leak in a shared wall can reach a neighbor. Call the line at any hour so we can isolate the right valve.

Rossmoor and retirement communities

Homeowners may need a plumber who is patient and clear. We explain the price before work starts.

Downtown, Rossmoor and the creek in tunnels

Walnut Creek itself runs through tunnels beneath downtown, and the Iron Horse Trail cuts north to south through the same core. Older downtown buildings sit on mixed infrastructure, and tenants in a mixed-use block should call the building owner as well as us when a riser fails.

Rossmoor, the 55-plus community of about 10,000 residents, uses its own rules for contractor work, so ask your management office what they require. We follow those rules and give you a clear price first.

Walnut Creek ZIP codes we serve

Our primary Walnut Creek ZIP is 94596, the downtown ZIP that includes the Broadway Plaza area. We also serve 94595, which includes Rossmoor, along with 94597 and 94598, so the whole city is covered by one 24/7 dispatch line.

Heather Farm to Lime Ridge: tree roots and sewer laterals

Open spaces like Heather Farm Park and the Lime Ridge, Shell Ridge and Acalanes Ridge areas ring the city, and mature trees sit on many lots. Roots follow moisture into laterals, which is why a camera check pays off before a remodel. See sewer inspection and the hydro jetting page for how we clean what the camera finds.

Work in Walnut Creek follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and Central San's specifications apply to sewer laterals within its service area.
