What Gregory Gardens is

Gregory Gardens is a residential neighborhood in the central part of Pleasant Hill. A public encyclopedia entry for the city describes Gregory Gardens as a 1950 subdivision. Pleasant Hill did not get a post office until 1948, and its population grew from 5,686 in 1950 to 23,844 in 1960, so most of the surrounding streets were laid out in a very short span.

Neighborhood maps place Gregory Gardens near Taylor Boulevard, Contra Costa Boulevard and Pleasant Hill Road, with Grayson Creek and the Contra Costa Canal close by. Those maps are informal and the edges are not drawn the same way twice, so if you are not sure whether your address counts, call and give us the street. We will take the call either way.

Why the age of the house matters, and what it does not tell you

A house built in the early 1950s may still have its original plumbing, may have been repiped, or may have a mix of both. The neighborhood name cannot tell you which. Owners change water heaters, fixtures and supply lines over seventy years, and some replace the sewer lateral. What matters is what is actually in your walls and under your yard.

That is why a first visit starts with questions and a look, not an assumption. We would rather confirm the pipe material and condition than tell you what an older home usually has.

Services homeowners here ask about

Slow or repeated clogs in an older line often point to something inside it, and drain cleaning with a camera look is the way to find out where. For a backup that returns, sewer line cleaning and an inspection tell you whether roots or a break are involved. A water heater that no longer keeps up may need water heater repair or a replacement quote, and an unexplained rise in the water bill is a reason to call about leak detection.

Who is responsible for the sewer lateral?

Central San, the district that serves Pleasant Hill, states that the property owner is responsible for the entire sewer from the house to where it connects to the main. It also holds permit authority and inspection jurisdiction for building sewer repairs in its service area. Confirm the permit step for your address before work starts.

A simple habit for older homes

  • Exercise the main shutoff valve once a year so it does not seize when you need it.
  • Replace old flexible supply lines to toilets and sinks when you see bulging, rust or crusted fittings.
  • Note any stain on a ceiling below a bathroom and photograph it with a date.
  • Keep the cleanout clear of dirt and plants so it can be reached.
