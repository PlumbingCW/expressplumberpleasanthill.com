Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Pleasant Hill, CA

A burst pipe can put hundreds of gallons into a house in minutes. Shut off the main water valve first, then call the Pleasant Hill line, which is answered 24/7. Turn off power to wet areas if you can reach the panel safely, and stay out of standing water near outlets.

Burst pipe repair is stopping the flow, cutting out the failed section and replacing it with new pipe and fittings, then testing the line and advising on drying and cleanup.

When a burst pipe is an emergency: the first five minutes

1

Shut off the main

Turn the main valve clockwise until it stops. In many homes it is near the meter, the street side of the house or the garage.

2

Open a low faucet

Draining the lines lowers pressure and slows the leak.

3

Kill power if wet

If water is near outlets or a panel, turn off the breaker only if you can do so without standing in water.

4

Call and document

Call us, then take photographs and video for your insurer.

If you cannot find or turn the main valve, say so when you call. We can talk you through alternatives, such as the curb stop at the meter box, which usually needs a special key.

Why pipes burst in this part of Contra Costa

Winter freezes are less common here than in colder states, so a burst is more often caused by corrosion, high water pressure, a failed joint or a pipe damaged during other work. Old galvanized steel can rupture at a thinned wall. Copper can fail at a pinhole that becomes a split.

We check the cause before we repair, because replacing a section without fixing a pressure problem just moves the failure down the line. Our repiping page explains when widespread corrosion calls for a larger replacement.

After the pipe is fixed

  • We test the line under pressure and check nearby fittings.
  • We tell you what to dry, and when drywall or insulation should come out.
  • We note the damage in a way your insurer can follow.
  • We advise whether a pressure regulator or repiping is worth considering.

Wet drywall and insulation can grow mold within a couple of days, so drying matters. We handle the plumbing, and a water damage restoration company handles drying and cleanup.

What affects burst pipe repair cost

We give a clear price before work starts. Factors:

Pipe locationAn exposed pipe in a garage costs less than one inside a finished wall.
Pipe materialCopper, PEX and galvanized each need different fittings.
Wall or ceiling accessOpening and patching adds time.
Extent of damageMultiple failures may point to larger replacement.
After hoursMidnight and holiday calls carry a separate rate.

Insurance often covers water damage from a sudden burst, though not always the pipe itself. Call your insurer early, and keep damaged items until they are documented.
