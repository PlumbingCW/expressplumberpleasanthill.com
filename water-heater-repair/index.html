Emergency Water Heater Repair in Pleasant Hill, CA

A water heater that quits on a cold morning or leaks onto the garage floor is an emergency, and our line is answered 24/7 in Pleasant Hill. If the tank is leaking, shut off the cold water valve above it, turn the gas control to pilot or the breaker off, and keep the area clear until the plumber arrives.

Water heater repair means finding why a tank or tankless unit is not heating or is leaking, and replacing the failed part such as a thermostat, element, burner assembly, gas valve or relief valve.

No hot water: what is usually wrong

No hot water is the most common call we take, and the cause depends on the fuel. On a gas tank, a pilot that will not stay lit often points to a worn thermocouple. On an electric tank, a tripped breaker or a failed heating element is the usual culprit.

We start at the controls and work toward the tank, testing before we replace anything. A part that costs little but is diagnosed correctly beats a tank that gets replaced for the wrong reason.

When a leaking tank is an emergency

Water pooling under the heater changes the call from repair to urgent. A leaking fitting or relief valve can be fixed the same day. A tank that leaks through its shell cannot be patched, because the steel liner has failed, and the answer becomes water heater replacement.

We tell you which it is before any work starts. If the leak is at the tank, we can set up a temporary shutoff so you keep cold water to the rest of the house, and we schedule the replacement, often the same day when stock and a crew allow.

Cold water valve

Turn the handle on the cold supply pipe above the heater clockwise until it stops.

Power or gas

Set electric units to off at the breaker. Set a gas control to pilot and never relight a unit sitting in water.

Open one hot tap

Opening a hot faucet upstairs breaks the vacuum and lets the tank drain more evenly if you decide to drain it.

Protect belongings

Lift boxes off the garage floor and take photos of the water for your records.

Gas, electric, hybrid and tankless repair

Gas and electric tanks make up most of the repairs we see around Contra Costa Boulevard, and the fixes are routine. Heat pump hybrid units add a compressor and air filter to the list, and they need clear space around the unit so the compressor can breathe.

Tankless units fail differently. Scale on the heat exchanger, a blocked intake or a flame sensor that needs cleaning will show up as an error code or lukewarm water. Our tankless water heater page covers flushing and descaling in detail.

Code details that matter on any repair

Water heater work in California follows the California Plumbing Code, and the City of Martinez's own water heater handout, which cites those code sections, is a useful snapshot of what inspectors look for. It lists seismic strapping with two straps and a third on tanks over 52 gallons, a temperature and pressure relief drain that ends 6 to 24 inches above the ground, and a gas shutoff valve beside the unit.

Permit rules differ from one city to the next, so ask the Pleasant Hill building division whether your repair needs a permit. We follow the handout's items on every job because they protect your home regardless of who inspects.

Anode rods and tank life

The anode rod is a sacrificial metal rod that corrodes so the tank does not. Checking it every few years can add life to a tank, and a rod that has worn to a thin wire is a warning that the liner is next.

If your heater is well past ten years old, we will price both the repair and replacement so you can decide with real numbers, not a sales pitch.
