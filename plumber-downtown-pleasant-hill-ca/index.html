A downtown built around two boulevards

Downtown Pleasant Hill is the city center, a mixed-use area built around Contra Costa Boulevard and Monument Boulevard. The shopping center called Downtown Pleasant Hill opened in July 2000 and was designed to resemble a small Main Street, and before that the city had no true downtown. Interstate 680 runs along the area's edge.

Public neighborhood profiles show a mix of housing types nearby, from detached houses to condos, townhomes and apartments. Shops, restaurants and offices sit among them.

Who is responsible when something leaks

In a condo or townhome, the split between what you own and what the association owns is set by your governing documents, not by plumbing habits. A supply line inside your unit is usually the owner's concern. A shared drain stack or a main serving several units is often the association's. The documents vary, so check yours or ask your manager before you pay for work on a shared line.

Shops, restaurants and offices

A food business has plumbing needs a house does not: grease traps and interceptors, floor drains, and equipment that cannot wait. If a kitchen drain backs up at service time, close the affected sinks and call right away. Our commercial plumbing page explains what we need to know when you call, and hydro jetting is the usual answer for a line that keeps clogging with grease or debris.

Services that come up in attached housing

Condo owners often call about a water heater replacement in a closet, a running toilet under toilet repair, or a slow kitchen line that a drain cleaning visit can clear. When the source of water is not obvious, leak detection finds it without opening every wall.

Before work starts in a condo

Tell your manager or association what you plan to do, ask whether shutting off the water affects other units, and ask whether a permit or notice is needed. Rules differ between buildings.
